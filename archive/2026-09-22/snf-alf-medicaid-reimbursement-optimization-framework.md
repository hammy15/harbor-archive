## Summary
The framework optimizes Medicaid reimbursement for SNF/ALF by enhancing MDS/CMI capture, cost-reporting, and specialty program qualifications, while aligning with regulatory and payer-mix strategies. This is crucial as under-coding and inadequate data integrity can significantly impact financial outcomes.

## Key Facts
Section GG under-coding depresses Nursing CMI more than under prior RUG systems. Ventilator/complex medical rates produce largest per-resident differentials. MDS data integrity under heightened scrutiny in PDPM transitions. Myers & Stauffer dominant rate-setting contractor. Managed LTSS penetration growing. Base Medicaid rates frequently below full reported costs.
